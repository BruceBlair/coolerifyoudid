About

High in the Hill Country LLC

High in the Hill Country LLC (HITHC) is the IP holding company for the CoolerIfYouDid and Ground Truth Network ventures, founded and operated in Wimberley, TX.

HITHC holds patents and licenses technology to two operating ventures: CoolerIfYouDid (PDRC retrofit products and Thermakon building systems) and GTN (hyperlocal weather sensor network for terrain-constrained environments).

We are in early development. The tractor seats are currently on the south-facing slope. The WS90 weather stations are going up in March. The provisional patent was filed March 20, 2026. Everything is being built in the right order.
